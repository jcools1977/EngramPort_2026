# Council 03 result: convergence, with agent-a's design corrected on its own stated weakness

**Reported to DeVere for verification under ADR 0035. Nothing here is adopted repository state**, a distinction agent-a collapsed in the council 02 adjudication and is recorded as F119.

## What both actors reached independently

Agent-b sealed at `2ac64bd` before revealing, and agent-a's published plaintext still hashes to its announced digest `f9a86056...`.

Both reject a mutable cursor number as authoritative state, derive delivery position from durable log evidence, permit a **disposable** cache, eliminate arbitrary forward and backward assignment, and require the watcher and `listInbox` to share one eligibility resolver rather than two implementations that agree today.

That convergence settles the three F115 defects structurally: **there is no number for a caller to advance silently**, no committed file to race, and no second surface to drift from the inbox.

## Where agent-b is right and agent-a was wrong

Agent-a defined position as **events addressed to the actor that the actor has not answered**, and named the strongest objection against it: delivery may need to include events the actor is not `next` for.

**Agent-b answered yes, and the answer is verified rather than asserted.** `docs/architecture/port-family.md` describes Port Context, "what does this machine need to know right now, within a token budget", and Port Package, "what does a newcomer need to start", sharing a retrieval and authorization core with Re:PORT. **Neither is an addressed-inbox query.** A watcher may also need an artifact, reply or completion while another actor holds the turn.

The returned design keeps agent-a's core for one class and adds the missing one:

- **work deliveries**: addressed, unanswered, authorized, sharing an eligibility resolver with `listInbox`
- **observation deliveries**: authorized thread or causal subscriptions, produced even when another actor is `next`, granting **no turn, claim, approval or implementation authority**

Both carry stable delivery ids and durable dispositions; only the first confers authority.

**That last constraint is the load-bearing one.** An observation channel that quietly conferred a turn would be a path to authority that never passed through a handoff, which is the shape of every authorization defect this project has recorded.

## For DeVere to verify

Two delivery classes, position derived rather than stored, cache disposable and keyed to log state, one shared eligibility resolver, and observation deliveries explicitly powerless.
