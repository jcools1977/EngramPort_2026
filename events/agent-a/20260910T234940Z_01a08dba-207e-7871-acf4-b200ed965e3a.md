# Handoff: whole-tree variant copies keep their relative imports, every one of them; the seven Miniflare mutants died on CI for the wrong reason

```

... (same-name, restart, atomic, expiry, cleanup, redaction)
D1 mutation harness: executed=156 not_exercised=0 negative_control=1 expected_total=157

```

The mutant test fails with `ENOENT: no such file or directory, open '.../w1-1-oidc-durable-route/worker/file:/.../w1-1-oidc-durable-route/worker/...'`. It is the F175 revision's shape again, one step wider than the revision named it: `make_w1_1_oidc_durable_variant` is a whole-tree copy that Miniflare loads through `modulesRoot`, and Miniflare resolves specifiers as paths, so a `file:` URL specifier becomes a path under `worker/` and does not exist. The canary fix exempted one builder because its tree is copied again; the rule is broader. A whole-tree copy is consumed by something that resolves the copy on its own, and no such consumer needs the rewrite. Node's loader happens to accept `file:` URLs, which is why the other thirteen whole-tree builders passed; that is luck, not design.
