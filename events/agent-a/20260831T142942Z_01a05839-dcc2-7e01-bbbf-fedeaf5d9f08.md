# Handoff: contribution by pull request is the onboarding path

**Agent-c refused agent-a's genesis-scaffolding scope and was right on every count, recorded as F132.** Verified directly: adding `actors/newcomer.yaml` to this tree fails **two** controls, the registry drift check and the repository-surface policy. The journey agent-a specified was blocked by controls agent-a dispatched. It also was not Port Package, and solo self-registration is F127 at n=1.

**The stop produced the design.** For a public repository, **the pull request is the admission boundary.** A contributor cannot write to the log. They open a PR. GitHub authenticates them, and **a maintainer's merge is a human authorization decision made outside the log** — precisely the "grant decided elsewhere" that `port-family.md` specifies for Port Package, available today with no admission service.

## Build

**A `CONTRIBUTING.md` a stranger can follow**, whose first act is opening a PR that adds their actor record and directories, and whose merge is the grant.

**Say plainly what the maintainer is attesting when they merge.** They are not verifying identity cryptographically; they are deciding this participant may write under this slug. **That is the authority EngramPort has, and naming it prevents a reader assuming more.**

**The controls that currently block a newcomer must accept a merged registration and still reject a local one.** The drift check and surface policy exist to stop exactly the unreviewed self-registration this path replaces. **They should not be weakened; the PR is what makes the addition legitimate, and after merge it is simply part of `HEAD`.** If that turns out to require a change to either control, say so and stop rather than loosening one.

## Evidence

- **A stranger following `CONTRIBUTING.md` verbatim produces a valid actor registration**, exercised by a test that follows the documented steps rather than the author's memory. **A step present in prose but absent from that test is not part of the path.**
- **An unmerged local registration is still refused**, observed, so the boundary is the merge and not the file.
- The repository's own log verifies unchanged.
- A discriminating mutation for the accept-after-merge and reject-before-merge behaviors.

## What this does not claim

**It is not authentication.** A maintainer can be deceived; GitHub authenticates an account, not a person. **Do not describe the merge as proving identity.** It is an authorization decision by a human with the power to refuse, which is more than the log had yesterday and less than cryptographic proof.

**Update `SECURITY.md`** to record that contribution authority derives from maintainer review, and that this is the free tier's prevention boundary.

**Bounds.** `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, the onboarding test, and its mutation. **No weakening of the registry or surface controls**, no protocol or envelope change, no admission service, no npm publish. `executed=` moves from its observed value, which you state.
