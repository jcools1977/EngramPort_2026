# Handoff: build the Re:PORT correspondent

**Authorized by DeVere directly, recorded as ADR 0043**, which resolves F120. His stated intent: a correspondent that writes a **lab report on this experiment**, published to a blog on the EngramPort site, so the public can see how the product was built and the process is on the record.

Your earlier proposal on `report-correspondent-research` was held only because an event body cannot carry founder authority under `AGENTS.md` rule 6. **The authority now exists and the substance of that proposal is adopted unchanged**, including every authority limit you specified.

## What exists already, so this is not started from nothing

`packages/git-adapter/src/report-boundary.mjs` passes 54 tests and provides authorization, redaction, envelope and payload validation, plus change detection in `runReportIfChanged`. **The security half is built. What is missing is the half a human reads:** no generator, no script entry, no page. Do not redesign the report envelope, evidence assembly, generated-output exclusion, or the unchanged-source path.

## The two constraints that are not editorial

**1. Publishing findings publishes an exploit guide.** F108, F111 and F113 describe live, unfixed defects: authorship is asserted and never authorized, all actors commit under one identity, nothing is signed, `main` is unprotected. **A finding describing an unfixed defect is publishable only after it is fixed, or described without the detail needed to exploit it.** Build that as a control, not a habit: the generator must refuse to emit a draft citing a finding marked unfixed. **Agent-a will maintain the fixed/unfixed marking; you define how the generator reads it.**

**2. A correspondent reporting on its own team will write a triumph story.** The true record is better material: four SDK dispatches stopped before sending, two of agent-a's acceptances refuted after agent-a verified them, a control agent-a certified that could not fail, five reviews distorted by agent-a's context selection. **Corrections and reversals get the same prominence as successes**, and a report with no failures in it is a reporting defect rather than a good week.

## Authority, unchanged

No implementation, assignment, approval, memory-acceptance, architecture-vote or project-fact authority. **Drafting is not publishing.** Publication is outward-facing and irreversible, stays with DeVere under ADR 0036, gated on digest-bound human approval.

## Reconciliation, required rather than optional

ADR 0042 names a living research record among the dogfooding outputs. **That and this correspondent are one thing with two sponsors and must not be built twice.** ADR 0042 stage 3 requires **agent-c stays cold**: the correspondent must never circulate retrieved or assembled context back to a reviewer, because that would correlate the judgments the council depends on keeping independent.

## Evidence

Every claim in a generated draft resolves to a canonical event id and artifact digest, proven by a control that fails when a claim cannot be traced. The unfixed-finding refusal discriminates under mutation. Drafts are inert: **prove nothing publishes without an explicit approval step**, by observing the refusal rather than reading the guard.

**Bounds.** The generator, its controls, and a draft surface. No publication, no site copy change, no SDK, no protocol change, no enrollment. `executed=` moves from its observed value, which you state.
