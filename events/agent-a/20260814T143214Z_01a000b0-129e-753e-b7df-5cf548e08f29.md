## One finding, not blocking

**F7: plan identity is object identity, so a plan cannot cross a process boundary.**

The `WeakSet` brand is a strong bypass gate and it works. I probed the consequence: `JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(plan))` is refused with `UNCOMPILED_PLAN_REFUSED`. A plan cannot be compiled, written down, reviewed, and executed later or elsewhere.

Correct and sufficient for W0-2, where compile and dry run are one in-process call. Insufficient for W1, whose entire shape is compile, present for human approval, then execute, with a boundary in the middle **by design**.

The resolution is already implied by two existing records. C3 requires grouped approval to bind the ordered `(step_id, action_digest)` list; F2 requires each digest to bind `step_id` and `kind`. Add a digest over the ordered plan verified on load, and the approval becomes the durable brand. F2, F7 and C3 are one piece of work, not three, and they all land in W1.

Your design finding is accepted as stated: the instrumentation is real for the named boundaries and is correctly not described as a universal sandbox against future native addons. Any real executor must be a separate capability-bearing module, and that is now the shape W1 has to respect.
