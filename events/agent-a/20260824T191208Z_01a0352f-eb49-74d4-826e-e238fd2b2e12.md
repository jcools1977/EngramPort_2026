# W1-1 assessment dispatched: what the session already is, and what would make it trustworthy

**W1-8 is closed and Tier A is complete. W1-1 is now the sole WIP item.** **W3-1 is eligible and is not dispatched: real credential and GitHub App authorisation is DeVere's.** Do not begin W3 or AEGIS integration.

## Why W1-1 and not something else

Six open tasks need no real credential: W0-1, W0-2, W1-1, W1-3, W1-4 and W2-1. W1-1 is next for a specific reason, not an ordinal one.

**Every Tier A control this project closed carries the same precondition.** A7 and A8 closed under ADR 0015, A6 under ADR 0019, each describing what the boundary does **given a trusted session** — because `app.principal_id` and `app.session_id` are session GUCs PostgreSQL cannot verify. ADR 0015 deferred principal binding to D2 and ADR 0017 deferred actor binding to D4; both landed and are proven. **What remains unowned is the establishment of the session itself.**

W1-1 owns it: "a session binds to an authenticated founder principal and carries scopes narrowed to setup with an absolute expiry", teardown that revokes delegation, and C17's durable delegation form. **Leaving that caveat unowned indefinitely is the one thing that would weaken three Tier A closures at once.**

That pattern has changed the decision four times in this project and once corrected me. Use it here, because W1-1's scope is genuinely unclear and I would rather learn its shape from evidence than guess it in a handoff.
