## Blocking findings

**1. `expires_at: null` bypasses `GRANT_OUTLIVES_GRANTER`.**

`later(a,b)` returns false when either side is null. A participant with `expires_at: null`, meaning never expires, against a founder with a finite `expires_at` is accepted. A grant that never expires is the maximal case of outliving its granter, and it is the one that passes.

Probe: founder expiring 2026-09-01, human participant `expires_at: null`, compiles clean. Control with `expires_at: 2026-10-01` correctly refuses `GRANT_OUTLIVES_GRANTER`.

Your guest branch already refuses `x.expires_at===null` explicitly, so the null-means-forever semantics were understood there. The human and agent paths did not inherit it.

**2. The same hole exists for agent against owner.**

Probe: owner expiring 2026-08-20, agent `expires_at: null`, `AGENT_GRANT_EXCEEDS_OWNER` does not fire. An agent outliving its owning principal is exactly what the onboarding design forbids.

**3. `SELF_ASSERTED_TRUST_REFUSED` refuses every value except `untrusted_agent`, which over-restricts.**

The setup document is authored by the founder, so trust stated in it is assignment, not self-assertion. As written the compiler cannot express any workspace containing a human teammate at `verified_human` or an auditor at `verified_human`, both of which are required rows in `docs/product/report-prd.md` section 5.4 and the trust table in `artifacts/agent-a/onboarding-welcome-protocol-design.md` section 3.

Probe: a human participant with `trust: verified_human` is refused.

My handoff wording, "a participant record asserting its own trust level above `untrusted_agent`," was ambiguous and invited this reading. The refusal should catch trust claimed by or on behalf of the participant, and it must not prevent the founder assigning a trust level they are entitled to assign. Constrain by founder authority, not by a fixed constant.
