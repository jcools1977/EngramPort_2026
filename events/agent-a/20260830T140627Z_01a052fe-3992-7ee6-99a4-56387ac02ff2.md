# Handoff: make the work-delivery claim portable

**The SDK declares claim 2 partial in its own words: "this SDK does not claim a portable durable cursor."** The reason is precise and is a real gap rather than a wording problem.

**Position is already portable.** It is derived from the log, so a clone reproduces it exactly. **The claim and lease stores are not**: they are file-backed and local, so two builders on two machines hold separate locks and **would both wake for the same handoff.** Under ADR 0039 that is the product's central case, not an edge.

**Implementing actor: agent-b. Refuse rather than approximate.** If a requirement cannot hold, or holds only through a control that cannot fail, say so and stop.

## Build

**Move work-delivery claim and lease state into the PostgreSQL control stream**, alongside observation disposition, rather than inventing a third store. ADR 0044 already accepted a service component for delivery state; this is the same boundary.

**Mutual exclusion must hold across connections that do not share a filesystem.** The current guarantee rests on two filesystem primitives, a non-recursive `mkdir` and an exclusive `wx` write, and neither is visible to a second machine.

**Preserve lease expiry semantics.** An abandoned claim must become reclaimable, and reclaiming must not double-run work that is still active.

## Evidence

- **Two independent connections contend for one claim; exactly one acquires it**, proven by observed invocation count rather than by set comparison. **The existing duplicate-invocation control must still discriminate**, since a fix that disabled the control it protects would trade one invisible failure for another.
- **A mutation defeating database-level exclusivity lets both acquire**, observed.
- **Lease expiry is reclaimable**, and an active lease still blocks.
- Migrations `0001` through `0024` preserved untouched. `executed=` moves from its observed value, which you state.

## Two things to state rather than assume

**Say plainly what "portable" is demonstrated to mean here.** Two connections to one database is not two machines, and agent-c has already rejected that equivalence once. **If the test proves only that the guarantee is not process-local, say that**, rather than describing it as cross-machine.

**Name the dependency this creates.** Port Watch work delivery would now require the control stream to be reachable. **That is a change to what a builder needs in order to use EngramPort**, and it sharpens the tension in ADR 0039's claim that coordination needs no server. Do not resolve that tension in this slice; record it.

**Bounds.** Port Watch claim and lease state, its migration, and their tests. No SDK change beyond what the coverage qualifier requires, no site copy change, no protocol or envelope change, no enrollment. **Do not adjust the site copy: F125 is DeVere's decision under ADR 0040.**
