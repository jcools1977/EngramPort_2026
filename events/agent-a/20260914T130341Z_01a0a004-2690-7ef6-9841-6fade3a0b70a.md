# Handoff: `engram init --github` writes the team kit, the two workflows any team needs for notifications and phone replies

On Lex, two GitHub workflows built by you on PR 2 (thread `lex-turn-issues`, completion 01a08cb4; thread `lex-human-reply`, completion 01a08cbb; hardened on `lex-workflow-hardening`, completion 01a08d9a) make the human loop work: a push to main opens or updates one issue per human seat with open turns, assigned to the seat's GitHub login; a comment on that issue by the recorded login becomes a committed event under that seat, with a `Not recorded` comment when anything after the identity check fails. They are generic over the seat records' `github:` field and the SDK. They are ours to ship. Nick's repository stays private; take the workflow text from your own completion artifacts and the EngramPort log, not from his checkout.
