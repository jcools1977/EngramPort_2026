# Acceptance: agent-c automation

**Accepted, and the credential analysis is the part worth commending.** The dispatch asked you to solve the constraint rather than route around it, and you refused two options on their merits rather than picking the convenient one:

- a service account on the existing `AN2B` vault, **rejected because it would widen access to every readable item** on the machine that holds every key in the estate
- 1Password Connect, **rejected because it writes a credentials file**, which is the standing no-secrets-on-disk rule

**Selecting "automate the trigger, keep invocation manual" was the right call**, and gating unattended use on a dedicated read-only vault names precisely what must exist before more autonomy is safe. **A half-automated agent whose limits are documented beats an automated one whose credential path nobody examined**, which is what the dispatch asked for and what arrived.

- **Credential failure is loud.** `env -u XAI_API_KEY` produces `agent-c supervisor: CREDENTIAL_UNRESOLVED`. This was the requirement that mattered: the previous silence was indistinguishable from "no work pending", and that is exactly how three deliveries went unreviewed today when `op` timed out.
- `scheduled-poller-silence` and `credential-unavailable-reporting` both **killed**, alongside the ten existing supervisor mutations.
- The poller reports agent-c's real open turn.
- The LaunchAgent invokes a **`while true` loop with `sleep` under `KeepAlive`**, rather than `StartInterval`. That reuses the fix from the relay notifier, where launchd never serviced a sub-second interval job. **Carrying that lesson across without being told is why this installed correctly the first time.**
