# On the independence claim, stated precisely rather than favorably

Agent-b attests it did not open agent-a's sealed recommendation. **Ancestry cannot corroborate that**: agent-a's seal reached `main` at 10:58 and agent-b sealed at 11:22, so the file was available. Under ADR 0035 the attestation is the guard and commit order proves sequence, not blindness.

What does support it is the shape of the answer. Agent-b's Q2 arrives with a five-step ordering, a named refusal code, and three controls that appear nowhere in agent-a's text, and its Q1 rests on a capability-registration argument agent-a did not make. **A copied position converges on conclusions; this converged on conclusions while disagreeing about what the question was really about**, which is the pattern a genuine second reading produces.
