## Scope

1. **The resolver.** Given an authenticated `principal_id` and **nothing else from the request**, return that principal's held authority from trusted datastore state. `start` takes no `founder_authority` argument. Caller-supplied scopes, roles, trust, tenant, project, or founder authority are **never** accepted as evidence, and the resolver's answer must be uninfluenceable by the setup payload, including through a tenant hint, project slug, or scope request.

2. **Atomic bootstrap.** Where no tenant exists, one transaction creates tenant, project, principal, and owner membership. Partial failure leaves nothing.

3. **Concurrency, enforced by the datastore.** Two genuinely concurrent founder attempts produce **exactly one winner**. The invariant is enforced by a uniqueness constraint or serializable isolation, **never** by an application pre-check, because a check-then-insert race is exactly what F13 records. The loser is deterministic: either refused with a distinct error or resolved onto the established tenant. Say which you chose and why.

**Genuinely concurrent** means two real PostgreSQL sessions with overlapping transactions, not two sequential calls. Show the transaction interleaving in your evidence.

4. **Session delegation** is bounded by the **resolved** founder authority and by durable session expiry under constraint C6, never by an in-memory sweep alone.

5. **The dispatch gate.** Evidence binds to the **exact current revision and digest** of `docs/security/setup-credential-threat-model.md`, which is **revision 8**, digest `629ae3f2654aba46e4c1158fc234c6b24831a369505ccf41878af3207b091089`. Missing, stale, wrong-revision, or incomplete Tier A evidence makes **W3-1 mechanically ineligible**. No waiver, flag, prose assertion, or task registration may satisfy a missing control; registering a task is not evidence that its controls passed.

Tier A is **A1 through A9**. Your task supplies A1 and A2; the gate must treat A3 through A9 as outstanding and therefore keep W3-1 ineligible today. A gate that passes now would be a gate that does nothing.
