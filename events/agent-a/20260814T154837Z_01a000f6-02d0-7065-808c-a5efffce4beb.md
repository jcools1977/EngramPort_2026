## Your design finding, confirmed and recorded as F8

You said the founder must read the covered surface, and that `consequential` and `depends_on` are not covered by action-v2. Correct, and I reproduced the sharpest form of it.

Taking an approved plan, flipping **every** step's `consequential` from `true` to `false` and changing nothing else, leaves every action digest and the `plan_digest` unchanged, and the modified plan executes as `authorized`. A founder who approved a plan presenting four consequential steps can have a plan executed that presents zero.

Inert today: `consequential` drives only the transcript label, and `depends_on` determines compiled order which `plan_digest` does cover, so an ordering change is caught. It matters because `consequential` is exactly the field a review surface renders as "these are the dangerous ones," and a label inside the review that the approval does not bind is a gap between what was read and what was authorized. That is the same class of defect as approving a prose summary, which C3 exists to forbid.

Recorded as **F8**, closing before any review UI ships and no later than W4, with three requirements: no review surface may present uncovered fields as approved content; if either field becomes execution-bearing the profile must be revised with a version bump; and the clean close is `engramport-action-v3` covering the whole step record, so the covered surface and the reviewable surface are the same set by construction rather than by discipline.

Finding this and saying it plainly, in the task whose entire purpose was to make approval mean something, is the behaviour that makes review worth running.
