# The reading is accepted and decided in ADR 0018. D4 implementation dispatched

**D4 remains the sole WIP item. A7 and A8 stay open.** Do not begin W1-8, W3 or AEGIS integration.

## The finding is accepted

Only the event was added. That is the third time in this thread a reading has arrived before an implementation, and each time it has been worth more than the code would have been.

Every schema claim checks out: `actor_kind` is `('human','agent','service')`, `trust_level` is `('system','verified_human','trusted_service','trusted_agent','untrusted_agent','imported')`, `agent_sessions` binds one actor to a tenant and project and **carries no principal**, and `actor_delegations` binds actor to principal with scopes. Your composed rule is right, and your reason for composing it — that "trusted service" alone would be a label claiming a narrower identity than the comparison enforces — is exactly the defect ADR 0017 named.

**Decisions are recorded in `docs/adr/0018-custody-minter-identity.md`**, digest `7e8c4a0fb8d8702e79c1bd70f44a18f260b326f1b880fb3878cbc59b67d9cbda`. All six of your rule's clauses are adopted as written, including refusing `trust='system'` for services on your reasoning: narrowness is recoverable, breadth is not.

## Four things I am adding, and one is the reason this is not a small slice

**1. The seed already contains the forbidden shape.** All three seeded actors are `kind='agent'`, `trust='trusted_agent'`, and `agent-a` is delegated to `11000000-…-0001` — **the exact principal every custody fixture mints as**. M8 is not hypothetical here; the system's minting principal is agent-delegated in the seed today. Your mutation is therefore close to the existing seed, and the **paired positive needs a new synthetic `service`/`trusted_service` actor**, because none exists.

**2. Enforce it in the database function, not only the adapter.** Tenant derivation, model derivation, scope containment and the class gate are all read inside `mint_custody_reference`; this joins them. An adapter-only check is bypassable by anything holding `engram_maintenance`.

**3. State the limit plainly in the evidence.** `app.session_id` is exactly as forgeable as `app.principal_id` — both are session GUCs set by the privileged role. **This does not solve what ADR 0015 deferred.** It narrows which identity may mint **given** a trusted session, which is what M8 asks and no more. Do not let the artifact imply otherwise.

**4. The blast radius is the hard part.** `mint_custody_reference` has **32 call sites** across `tests/`, `scripts/` and `packages/`, and **no fixture seeds an `agent_sessions` row today** — the count is zero. Every minting fixture in D1, D1F, D2, D3, the canary and the mutation harness must gain an actor, a session and a delegation.

**No accepted control's observed outcome may change.** Produce a **before-and-after outcome table** covering every affected fixture, and if any control's outcome moves, **return it as a finding rather than fixing it silently**. That instruction is what made the M3 slice trustworthy, and it matters more here because the surface is an order of magnitude larger.

Also record that **delegation revocation is by row removal** — `actor_delegations` has no `revoked_at`, only `expires_at` — so no evidence implies a flag that does not exist.

## ADR 0016's project-context deferral is NOT discharged

You reported the reachability without claiming it, which was right. **The determination is: not discharged.** Using `agent_sessions.project_id` to disambiguate a multi-membership principal would let session state select authority, and that needs its own decision rather than arriving as a side effect. **Ambiguous membership continues to refuse.** Report the reachability again if you like; do not use it.

3. **The discriminating mutation, exactly as you proposed**: give the seeded `trusted_agent` the custody scope and a session, remove **only** the kind and trust refusal, and observe the agent-backed mint **succeeding** with the forbidden actor id in `custody_rows.minted_by_actor_id`. Leave derivation, delegation and recording intact — your reasoning that a broader mutation would conflate identity with authority is correct and is why this one isolates M8.
4. **The paired positive**: a new synthetic `service`/`trusted_service` actor with an active session, an exact live delegation and matching tenant and project mints normally and is recorded.
5. **Negative controls**, each with its own named outcome: absent or ended session; `kind='agent'` at every trust level including `system`; `kind='service'` at every trust level except `trusted_service`; a `trusted_service` lacking the exact delegation; and one whose session or delegation tenant or project does not match.
