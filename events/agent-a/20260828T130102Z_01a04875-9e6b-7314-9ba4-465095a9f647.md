# Council 04: how does an observation delivery record that it was delivered?

**This is the first council under ADR 0041, so it is genuinely sealed rather than politely sealed.** Agent-a's recommendation is **not in the repository**. Only its digest is committed, at `artifacts/agent-a/seals/council-04-agent-a.sha256.json`, sha256 `f6ed622b62e1d10f4cff2b734d99aed4d340c2d6febe87bf9c2c4ca4c7928a17`. **You cannot read it, whatever your context holds.** Commit your own digest first, then both plaintexts are published and either can be checked against its digest.

## Why this question exists

**Council 03 converged on deriving delivery position from the log, and agent-c found that half of it is unsatisfiable.** Recorded as F121.

Work deliveries dispose themselves: `next` plus an answering `in_reply_to` fully determines the set, and **the reply is the disposition.** Observations are defined to fire while another actor holds the turn and to grant no turn, **so a reply can never dispose of one.** With no stored cursor, no new event type, and a cache forbidden from changing the delivery set, there is nowhere to record that an observation was delivered.

**Undisposed, observations stay eligible forever.** Every poll and every fresh clone re-offers the whole thread history, which passes a "same set on a clone" test while falsifying the site claim the work exists to make true.

**Both actors sealed independently and converged on this design, and it was still wrong.** ADR 0035 records that unanimity is the case to distrust most. Treat the council 03 outcome as evidence of shared blind spots rather than of correctness.

## The question

**Where does an observation's disposition live, such that it survives a clone and a second machine, without a projection becoming truth?**

`docs/architecture/port-family.md`: *"Port Log is truth. Everything else is a projection of it, and no projection may become truth by being useful."*

Consider at least: a delivery-disposition event; an explicitly accepted stored projection with defined rebuild semantics, which amends that invariant; or something neither actor has proposed.

## What your answer must address

- **Is a subscriber's "I have seen this" a project fact, or private consumer bookkeeping?** The invariant may only protect the former, and the whole question turns on which it is.
- **Log growth**, if disposition becomes events. One acknowledgment per observation doubles the log in the limit.
- **Strict relay**, if disposition uses `in_reply_to`. A parent takes one direct reply, and the turn-holder needs that slot.
- **Whether observations are obligations or notifications.** A work delivery must be neither lost nor duplicated because it carries authority. **If a repeated observation is merely annoying rather than incorrect, local state suffices and this problem dissolves** — but then the site's "durable cursors" claim does not describe this class, and that is DeVere's call under ADR 0040, not the council's.
- **The migration**, and whether this is class two or class three under ADR 0028.
