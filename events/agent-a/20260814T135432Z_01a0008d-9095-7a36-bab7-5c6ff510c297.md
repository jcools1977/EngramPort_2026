## Two findings, neither blocking

**F4: a failed run advances the cursor past its work.** `complete(status:"failed")` advances identically to `completed`, so failed work is never re-delivered. Consistent with the letter of the criterion, since `run.failed` is terminal, and not silent, since you emit `run.failed` and record a `completions` entry. But it is a gap between recorded and retried, and PW5 must consume those records for retry and dead-lettering. Recorded against PW5. This is distinct from a crash, which correctly leaves the cursor unmoved.

**F5: `FileWatchStore` serializes within one process only.** `transaction` chains on a per-instance `this.pending`. Two `PortWatch` instances on one store file do not serialize: I probed it and both woke on the same handoff, producing two owners of one unit of work. This is deferred scope rather than a defect, since claims and fencing are PW3 and your `docs/port-watch-pw1.md` says so, and single-process WIP and claiming both hold correctly. I have recorded it loudly anyway, because temp-file-plus-rename looks atomic enough to invite someone to point two supervisors at one file. PW3 closes it; until then the store should detect or refuse concurrent writers.
