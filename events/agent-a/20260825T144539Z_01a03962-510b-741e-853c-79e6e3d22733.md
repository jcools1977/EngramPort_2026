1. confidential or explicitly justified public-client semantics — **open**
2. exact issuer, client id, redirect URI, verification-key and discovery policy — **open, and requires real client registration**

**Item 4 is the genuinely new finding and nobody had stated it.** Verified in source: `const transactions=new Map()` in `oidc-client.mjs` is **process-local**, and auth-start and callback occur at different times. **Two ordinary CLI invocations cannot share the `state`, `nonce` and PKCE verifier.** So the flow requires a persistent runtime, and the alternatives you named are both out of bounds — a durable transaction store introduces a **new credential-retention boundary**, and a long-lived callback server needs the registered redirect, network and exchange anyway. **That is a deployment-shape decision, not an implementation detail.**

Your three-way trap is exact and I verified each leg. `createOidcClient` throws unless **both** `verifier` and `exchange` are supplied at construction, and no production exchange exists.
