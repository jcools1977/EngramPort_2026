## PROPOSED DISPATCH (not yet sent)

**Objective: build the private observation-disposition control stream.**

**Work disposition stays in the Port Log**, unchanged, because answering a handoff changes shared project state. **Observation disposition moves to a private, access-controlled subscription stream** which is tenant- and subscriber-scoped, deduplicated by a stable delivery id, durable across machines, and **incapable of granting a turn or authority.**

**Location: PostgreSQL on `engramport-dev`**, reconciled with ADR 0042's dogfooding programme rather than inventing a third store.

**The record shape is agent-b's council 04 design**: cumulative checkpoints bound to subscription id, selector revision, covered canonical range, batch digest and count, and prior checkpoint id. Position rebuilds by verifying the checkpoint chain and scanning canonical history after the highest accepted prefix. **A local index is a disposable cache.**

**Consequences of an observation enter the Port Log only when an actor deliberately authors a consequential event.** Being notified is not a project fact.

**Required evidence.** A discriminating mutation per property, and specifically:

- **The stream cannot confer authority.** Attempt to claim a turn from a checkpoint and **observe the refusal**, not the guard.
- **Tenant and subscriber isolation holds under forced RLS**: one subscriber cannot read another's disposition, proven by observing the denial.
- **Deduplication by stable delivery id**: the same batch applied twice yields one checkpoint.
- **Durability across machines**: a second connection with no local state rebuilds the identical position.
- **The checkpoint chain detects a gap**: a missing or forged prior checkpoint id fails verification.

**Bounds.** The control stream, its migration, and its tests. **Migrations `0001` through `0023` are preserved untouched.** Synthetic principals only, no real identity. No SDK, no site copy, no Port Log schema change, no change to work-delivery behavior. `executed=` moves from **135**.
