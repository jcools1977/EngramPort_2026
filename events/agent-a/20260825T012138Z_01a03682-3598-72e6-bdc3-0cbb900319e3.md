# The gap is real and verified; the crypto slice is NOT blocked by it

Right to stop. **Every claim verified against source**: `start` accepts only `{principal_id}`; `principals` is unique per **tenant** on `(tenant_id, external_issuer, external_subject)`; `founder_authorities` is `principal_id uuid PRIMARY KEY` with **no external identity column at all**; `bootstrap_workspace` writes the synthetic pair `('bootstrap', p_principal_id::text)`; and **no global issuer/subject resolver exists anywhere in `migrations/`**.

**Your framing understates it slightly, in your favour.** This is not only a missing mapping — it is **the root of the trusted-session caveat carried on A6, A7 and A8**. Founder authentication happens before a tenant exists, so the one place external identity is stored is the one place that cannot yet be reached. The "authenticated founder" fact has always been injected, and this is why.

ADR 0026, digest `c42334cbb5a76b317cbf42d69f28c3b0678a2ff71a1065c0c7cce76cce235490`.

## Decided

**The verifier and the binder are separate components.** The cryptographic verifier **never returns a `principal_id`** — it returns verified claims or refuses. A separate binder maps verified claims to an internal principal, and only the binder's output crosses the authenticator interface. The accepted identity-surface control is untouched.

**This unblocks your whole cryptographic slice.** Your finding treats it as blocked on the mapping; **it is not, once the seam sits there.** Signature, key rotation, algorithm confusion, issuer, audience, expiry and nonce are all provable against synthetic fixtures without knowing which principal the claims resolve to.

**The binding is a registry lookup, never a derivation.** Your rejections 1 and 2 are adopted verbatim: `sub` is issuer-scoped and opaque and drops the issuer half, and a derived UUID has **no namespace, canonicalisation, collision, migration or recovery rule** in this repository — and a derivation with no recovery rule is unfixable once wrong.

**Registry shape**: a forward-only relation binding verified `(issuer, subject)` to `founder_authorities.principal_id`, **globally unique on `(issuer, subject)`** because no tenant exists to scope it; populated out of band; never writable by the authenticating path; forced RLS; reachable only through a `SECURITY DEFINER` resolver. **The resolver ignores any caller-supplied principal id**, exactly as `create_setup_session_delegation` ignores `p_asserted_founder_principal_id` — that pattern is accepted twice already and is reused rather than reinvented.

**Four refusals, none folded**: `FOUNDER_BINDING_ABSENT`, `FOUNDER_BINDING_AMBIGUOUS`, `FOUNDER_BINDING_DISABLED`, `FOUNDER_BINDING_CONFLICT`. **Ambiguity fails closed**, per ADR 0016.

## Not decided, and not by me

**Whether one external identity may found more than one tenant**, and if so whether it gets one principal id or distinct tenant-local ids. That decides whether founder identity is global or per-tenant, which is a **product** question about tenancy rather than an engineering one. Both answers are implementable and they produce different registries and different uniqueness. It is DeVere's, and so is carrying the verified issuer and subject into `principals` at bootstrap, since that **changes `bootstrap_workspace`, an accepted function**.

**So the binder stays blocked and criterion 1 stays open regardless** — its real half needs an identity provider DeVere has not authorised.

## Dispatched: the cryptographic verifier, synthetic fixtures only

Exactly the previous handoff's list, now unblocked by the seam. Nothing reaches a network; no real provider, credential or tenant.

1. **Signature verification against a synthetic JWKS, including key rotation** — a token signed by a retired key refused, one signed by the current key accepted.
2. **Algorithm confusion refused**: `alg: none`, and an RS256 public key presented for HS256 verification. **These two are what turn a verifier into a rubber stamp**, so both fail closed and both get their own mutation.
3. **Issuer, audience, expiry and nonce refused independently**, so no check carries another's weight.
4. **The verifier returns verified claims and never a `principal_id`**, and retains neither the raw token nor the claims beyond the call. Prove the non-retention rather than asserting it.

Paired positive and one discriminating mutation each. `executed=` moves from 72 on observed execution.
