# Handoff: layer 1, the contribution ledger

**Recorded as ADR 0047, refined by DeVere: participants share capacity, not money.** A subscription holder points their agent at the project; someone without a subscription contributes metered API spend. **Both are contributions to the same goal, measured differently and never conflated.**

**This is queued behind five open items and is not urgent. Take it in order.**

## The constraint that shapes the design

**Cost per event is knowable for metered actors and unknowable for subscription actors.** Agent-c's reviews carry exact `token_use` and `provider_cost` because the xAI API returns usage. Agent-a and agent-b are subscription-based and **cannot see a per-action token count.** DeVere and his collaborators will mostly be in the second category.

## Build

**A contribution ledger over the existing log**, showing per participant:

- **Work contributed by every actor**: events authored, handoffs accepted, completions with criteria coverage. **This is derivable from the log today and needs no new data.**
- **Metered spend for actors that have it**, read from the existing per-review `provider`, `model`, `token_use` and `provider_cost` fields.

**Do not invent a common unit.** The question the ledger answers is **"who is carrying the load"**, not "who owes what."

## The control that matters more than the report

**The ledger must refuse to present subscription contribution as a currency figure.** Amortizing a monthly subscription across a participant's events would produce numbers that look precise and are fiction. **This project has recorded that failure a dozen times**, most recently in F125 where copy named a mechanism it had already replaced.

**Make that a control with a discriminating mutation:** attempt to render a subscription actor's contribution in dollars and observe the refusal; remove the guard and show the fiction is emitted.

## Evidence

- The ledger reports agent-a, agent-b and agent-c over the real log, and **agent-c's figures reconcile with the 17 review artifacts**, currently $1.26 and 308,833 tokens.
- A subscription actor shows work contributed and **no currency figure**, observed.
- The dollars-for-subscription mutation is refused.
- The repository's log verifies unchanged. `executed=` moves from its observed value, which you state.

**Bounds.** The ledger and its controls. **No envelope or protocol change** — contribution is derived from what the log already holds. No pooling, no grants, no budgets: **those are layer 2 and must not be started here.** No site copy change.
