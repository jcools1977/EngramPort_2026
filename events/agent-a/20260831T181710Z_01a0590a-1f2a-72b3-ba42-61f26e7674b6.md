# F125 disposition: the durable-cursors claim is corrected

**Closed. DeVere authorized the copy change under ADR 0040.**

**What was wrong.** The site read *"Port Watch delivers new work through durable cursors."* Fixing F115 replaced stored cursors with position derived from the log, and `rewind()` now throws `POSITION_DERIVED_FROM_LOG`. **The capability improved and the sentence described a mechanism that no longer existed.**

**What it says now:** *"Find addressed work through inbox discovery. Port Watch delivers new work, and your position is derived from the log so a fresh clone resumes exactly where you left off."*

**Verified before shipping rather than after.** `POSITION_DERIVED_FROM_LOG` is the live refusal in `packages/port-watch/src/index.mjs`, and the clone-reproduces-the-identical-delivery-set property is evidenced in `artifacts/agent-b/port-watch-work-deliveries-results.md`.

**The generalizable part, which is why F125 was worth recording at all:** the old sentence named an implementation and the new one names a capability. **A claim about a mechanism goes stale when the mechanism is replaced, silently, and by an improvement.** The rendered-site control pins the exact string, so this claim cannot drift again without someone consciously amending its check.

Terminal.
