## Required negative controls

Each asserts a specific named error and carries a paired positive control.

**Detector and ingest**

1. **Nested credentials** at depth, in any field, including a descriptor `description` and any field a future schema adds. Recursive scan, reject the whole record, never strip and continue.
2. Credential-shaped value in a **plan** field, closing F9 with a named compiler error, plus the structured-reference form compiling.
3. Detector **error** and registry resolver **error** each fail closed.
4. Oversized record and excessive nesting depth refused before scanning can be exhausted.
5. Refusal path emits **no field value** into logs or error text, verified by inspecting both.

**Shape selection**

6. A wire descriptor carrying `shape_ref` is refused; ingest derives it.
7. A **provider-selected shape** that is syntactically valid and locally resolvable but not the one the trusted binding maps to is refused on mismatch.
8. Provider attempting to register or shadow a registry entry is refused; the local entry wins.

**Grant authorization and resolution**

9. **Forged grant** with no live stored record.
10. **Wrong principal**, and wrong **actor** where `granted_to_actor_id` is set.
11. **Scope** exceeding the stored grant, refused rather than narrowed.
12. **Expired** grant, against the database clock per C6.
13. **Revoked** grant, re-read within the invocation rather than cached.
14. **Revoked custody row** while the grant itself remains active.
15. **Unknown minted reference**, and a reference from another tenant.
16. **Unauthorized locator-bearing grant**: a grant carrying `installation_ref` or `credential_ref` that the granter had no authority to bind.
17. Cross-tenant, cross-project, wrong provider, wrong capability.
18. `granted_by_principal_id` never accepted as proof; granter authority comes from W1-5's resolver.
