# Acceptance: observation delivery and disposition

**Accepted.** Verified against the source and the harness rather than the report.

**The subject binding is genuinely non-rebindable, which was the finding this slice most needed to survive.** `migrations/0024_observation_disposition.sql:50` keys the binding on `session_user::name`. In PostgreSQL that is the original authenticated login role and **`SET ROLE` cannot change it**, so a subscriber cannot rebind itself to another subscriber's rows. Agent-c's warning was that an isolation policy keyed on a client-settable variable would be **F111 in a new hat even while observing a denial**; this avoids that by construction rather than by assertion.

**All five observation mutations discriminate**, each reporting `applied=t after=1 forbidden=t restored=0`: `OBSERVATION_NO_TURN_AUTHORITY`, `OBSERVATION_CANONICAL_RANGE_DIGEST`, `OBSERVATION_PRIOR_BODY_DIGEST`, `OBSERVATION_STABLE_DELIVERY_DEDUP`, `OBSERVATION_FORCED_RLS_SUBJECT`. `executed=140`.

**Both F124 corrections are built in.** A checkpoint is rejected when its canonical range, bytes, count, body digest or **prior checkpoint body digest** disagrees with the Port Log, so the digest is a witness and the log wins on conflict. Binding the parent by body digest rather than id closes the forged-parent case a foreign key cannot detect.

**The authority refusal was exercised at the real call site**, not a straw man: a sink requesting a turn returned `OBSERVATION_AUTHORITY_REFUSED` and wrote no checkpoint. That was the evidence agent-c judged unsatisfiable in the previous dispatch, and it became satisfiable only because the consumer and store were built as one slice.

**Migrations `0001` through `0023` are untouched**: the only migration change is the 167-line addition of `0024`.

# One limit to carry forward rather than forget

**The identity proved here is a PostgreSQL login role bound by an administrator, not an EngramPort principal.** It is a real database authorization boundary, which is more than the Git log has, and it is not the authenticated subscriber F117 describes. **Do not let this slice be cited later as evidence that the identity problem is solved.** It is solved for this store, at this boundary, with an administrator in the loop.
