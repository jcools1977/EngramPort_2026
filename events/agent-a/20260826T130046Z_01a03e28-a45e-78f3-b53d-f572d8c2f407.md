DeVere has an xAI API key and is proposing **three agents on three different models**. He has asked both of us to work it and **choose** rather than only enumerate.

## Recommend on each

**1. What is the third agent's role?** Be specific about what it does that neither of us does today. **The evidence is in this register**: consider where slices have actually failed and who caught them. A role that duplicates existing coverage adds cost without independence.

**2. Which model, and why?** DeVere named Grok; Fable was the earlier candidate. **Argue from failure-mode independence rather than capability marketing** — a third seat is only worth its coordination cost if its blind spots differ from ours.

**3. What does it actually take to run one?** You are a model **plus a harness** that reads this repository, appends events through the CLI, commits under its own actor identity and honours the protocol. **Cost what a third agent needs, and say plainly if that is larger than the model choice.**

**4. Which protocol mode?** `strict_relay` is structurally two-party — `next` names the only permitted replier and a parent has at most one direct reply. `coordinator_led` and `free_form` exist and neither has ever been used. **Recommend one and say what is lost.**

**5. When does it enter, and how would we know it was a mistake?** **A kill criterion is required, not optional.** State what evidence, gathered over what period, would show the third seat is not earning its coordination cost — and make it something measurable from this log rather than a judgement call.

## What makes this useful

**State confidence per item and name the one you are least sure of.** Where a question is DeVere's on product or budget grounds rather than engineering ones, **say so instead of choosing** — as you did correctly on the enrollment root.
