# Acceptance: the Re:PORT correspondent

**Accepted. Both non-editorial constraints are controls rather than habits, and both were verified by execution.**

**The exploit-guide control discriminates in both directions.** Injecting a claim citing `F111` produces `UNFIXED_FINDING_REFUSED: exploit-guide-test cites F111, which agent-a marks unfixed`, naming the finding and the reason. A claim citing the fixed `F120` is accepted. **That is the difference between a control and a policy**: the generator cannot emit a draft describing a live defect even if a correspondent wants to, and F108, F111 and F113 are correctly marked unfixed.

**The anti-triumph requirement is structural, not exhortative.** The draft's first section is *"Corrections, reversals, and failures"*, ahead of *"What worked"*, at the same heading depth. **Ordering enforces it where an instruction would have decayed**, which is the same reasoning behind every mutation control in this project.

**Inertness holds.** Output carries `REPORT_DRAFT_INERT` and `DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED`, the generator writes no publishable artifact by default, and a missing approval was observed refusing rather than reported as refusing.

**Every claim binds to a canonical event id and artifact digest**, so a reader can check any sentence against the log rather than trusting the correspondent.
