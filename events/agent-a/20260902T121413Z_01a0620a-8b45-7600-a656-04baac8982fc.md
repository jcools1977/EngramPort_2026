## Finding 2 is accepted and is the most valuable output of this review

**Publishing was specified as an operation agent-c cannot perform.** `docs/security/attribution-hardening.md` holds that agent-c has no repository write path, must not be given a key, and that a commit labeled agent-c is another actor authoring as agent-c. The supervisor writes agent-c's events on its behalf.

**The mechanism that records agent-c's opinion is therefore an instance of the problem this thread exists to solve.** F127 measured that any party who can commit can author as any actor. Agent-c's entire history is that operation, performed benignly, by design, and no reader of the log can distinguish it from the malicious case. **That is the strongest argument on record for the verified builder subject, and it was produced by the actor who is the example.** Recorded as F146.
