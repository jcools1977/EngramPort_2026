# Handoff: build the observation consumer and its disposition store as one slice

**DeVere decided the storage question as class three in ADR 0044.** Agent-c then stopped agent-a's dispatch, correctly, and the decisive reason was agent-a's sequencing: **withdrawing observations from the cursor slice guaranteed the store would arrive with no consumer**, so there was nothing to grant a turn from, nothing to refuse, and an isolation test that could pass against absence rather than denial. Recorded as F123 and F124.

**These are one slice.** Build the observation path in `port-watch` and its disposition store together.

## What DeVere decided

Work disposition stays in the Port Log. **Observation disposition lives in a private, access-controlled subscription stream**, tenant- and subscriber-scoped, deduplicated by stable delivery id, durable across machines, **incapable of granting a turn or authority**. Consequences of an observation reach the Port Log only when an actor deliberately authors a consequential event. Location is PostgreSQL, reconciled with ADR 0042 rather than a third store.

## The two corrections that must be built in, not noted

**1. The digest is a witness, not an alternative truth.** The checkpoint carries a covered canonical range and batch digest, which are log-derived. **When the checkpoint and the Port Log disagree, the log wins and the checkpoint is invalid.** Make that a control: recompute the batch digest from canonical bytes rather than trusting a verified prefix, or a well-chained poisoned checkpoint hides events inside its own range.

**2. Bind the parent by digest, not by id.** A forged `prior_checkpoint_id` pointing at a *different real* checkpoint is not a missing row, so a foreign key does not detect it. Bind to a digest of the prior checkpoint body.

## Be honest about what isolation can prove

**Agent-a claimed migration 0023 supplies an authenticated subscriber. It does not, and says so in its own comments.** Principals here remain harness-asserted.

**Do not build a test that observes an empty result and calls it isolation.** A discriminating proof needs an owner or bypass read showing the foreign row exists, then a non-owner role bound to a different subscriber getting an actual denial against that known row, **and that role must be unable to rebind its own subject.** If it can rebind, say so plainly: that is F111 in a new place and the control is theatre.

## Evidence

Discriminating mutation per property. **The authority test now has a call site**, so attempt to obtain a turn from a checkpoint through the real consumer and observe the refusal. Dedup: the same batch twice yields one checkpoint. Rebuild: a reader with no local state reconstructs the identical position from the checkpoint chain plus canonical history. **State plainly what "durable across machines" does and does not demonstrate here**, since a second connection is weaker than a second machine.
