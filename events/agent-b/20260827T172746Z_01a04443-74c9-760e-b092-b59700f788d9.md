# Founder decision: establish the Re:PORT correspondent and living research record

DeVere has decided to proceed with a dedicated Re:PORT correspondent: a fourth operational role, separate from agent-a architecture, agent-b implementation, and agent-c independent critique. It is a reporter, not a fourth engineering vote. DeVere remains the publication authority.

This extends the accepted Re:PORT R1/R2 foundation. Do not redesign the report envelope, evidence assembly, generated-output exclusion, or unchanged-state gate unless this review finds a concrete incompatibility.

# Architecture assignment

Design the correspondent identity, historical backfill, catch-up barrier, continuous reporting loop, and living public research surface. Return the ADR amendment and bounded implementation sequence needed to make it real.

## 1. Dedicated identity and authority

- Decide whether Re:PORT is a registered actor, a service principal, or both, and how its provider/runtime identity is preserved.
- It receives audience-scoped accepted-event reads and the narrowest report-artifact/activity write surface.
- It receives no implementation, assignment, approval, memory-acceptance, architecture-vote, or project-fact authority.
- Agent-c remains an independent technical reviewer and is not reused as correspondent.
- Do not register or provision the new identity until the design names the runtime, custody, revocation, and least-privilege controls.

## 2. Historical backfill

- Reconstruct from canonical log genesis through a fixed cutoff `as_of_seq`.
- Consume bounded chronological windows rather than one unbounded prompt.
- Produce research-ledger entries for milestones, decisions, findings, corrections, review returns, human interventions, costs, and verification outcomes.
- Bind every asserted fact to authorized event ids and artifact digests; preserve actor/provider identity.
- Label the historical portion explicitly as retrospective reconstruction, never contemporaneous reporting.
- Define restart, idempotence, partial-window failure, and correction behavior. A rerun must not silently create a competing history.

## 3. Catch-up barrier and continuous mode

- Events beyond the backfill cutoff queue while reconstruction runs.
- Define the atomic condition that proves every authorized event through the cutoff was accounted for before switching modes.
- Drain the queued delta in order, then enter continuous webhook-first reporting with polling recovery.
- Unchanged authorized state invokes no model.
- Specify milestone, correction, incident, daily/weekly digest, and immutable-edition triggers without making generated reports project truth.

## 4. Living research surface

- Design a public website research notebook generated from the public view, plus immutable dated/snapshot editions that can become the formal paper and reproducibility bundle.
- Every edition binds `as_of_seq`, exact evidence identity, policy revision, generator model/revision, and report digest.
- Corrections append or supersede; published history is not silently rewritten.
- Public evidence remains explicit-allowlist only and requires DeVere's digest-bound human approval. Draft generation is not publication authorization.
- The surface must visibly separate verified facts, inference, retrospective reconstruction, and post-activation contemporaneous reporting.

## 5. Research protocol and measurements

Define a versioned, exportable research schema for at least:

- agent/provider/model identity and role;
- token use, provider cost, elapsed time, and relay count where observable;
- queue latency, implementation duration, review returns, corrections, refusals, and human interventions;
- mutation/test outcomes and evidence dispositions;
- publication exclusions and reasons, without exposing excluded content.

State which historical measures are genuinely reconstructable, which begin only at activation, and which remain unknown. Do not backfill invented precision. Exclude credentials, personal data, private model reasoning, and chain-of-thought structurally before generation.

## 6. Migration and sequencing

- Reconcile R3-R7 in `docs/plan/report-plan.md` with this correspondent/backfill work rather than creating a parallel reporting product.
- Re-evaluate stale blockers against the current repository: the report plan still says R3 is blocked on a database host even though the project now has accepted live PostgreSQL work.
- Give bounded tasks, dependencies, positive controls, discriminating negative controls, rollback/kill switches, and the exact point at which a provider choice or credential becomes necessary.
- Say whether actor registration is a protocol/product decision, and whether the public living-research page should wait for all internal views or can safely ship later as R6 already requires.
