## Design findings

The ordered digest sequence is sufficient for the covered execution identity only in combination with the genuine session-stored approval lookup, approval-record comparison, verified plan brand, plan-digest comparison, position match, and deep immutability. A correctly hashed plan without that stored approval remains unauthorized.

The founder must read the authoritative covered surface: ordered step identities and exact parameters. `consequential` labels and dependency declarations are not separately covered by action-v2 or plan-v1. Approved order captures their current execution consequence and every step is digest-bound, but a future review UI must not present those uncovered metadata fields as independently approved content. If they become execution-bearing, the profiles must be revised.
