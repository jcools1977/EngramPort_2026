## Design findings

Within this component, “no identity outlives a session” is structural rather than a revocation-flag convention: the private live-session record is the sole container of the founder binding, delegation, and approvals, and teardown deletes that entire record. Credentials never enter it. A process loss is fail-closed because live authority and approvals are memory-only.

A future durable/provider-backed implementation must retain atomic deletion and server-enforced expiry; this commit makes no claim that an external store or real provider already has those properties. W0 scope/expiry predicates are exported and reused rather than reimplemented.
